      How the map was made
      1. Each populated 1 km cell is checked against the nearest Ookla mobile speed test from October to December 2024. A test within 2 km at the National Broadband Plan's speed (25 Mbps in towns, 10 Mbps in rural areas) means the cell is served. Slower tests nearby mean the signal is there but too slow. A town with no test nearby needs measuring first. A rural area with no test nearby is a rural gap: no evidence of broadband, which is not proof of no signal.
      2. For rural gaps only, a maximal covering search adds clinics and schools (public and private, every level) one at a time, each time choosing the host that reaches the most people not yet reached within 5 km. Greedy selection is guaranteed to reach at least 63% of the best possible coverage.
      3. "Poorest first" counts only people in the poorer 40% by Meta's Relative Wealth Index when choosing sites.
